# Information Architecture

## V1 Navigation

| Navigation item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Home | Introduce who I am and highlight my current work |
| Projects | Present completed, ongoing and planned engineering projects |
| Writing | Collect technical articles, learning notes and other writing |
| About | Describe my experience, interests and learning direction |
| Resume | Provide access to my professional résumé |

GitHub and LinkedIn will be available through the header or footer. A separate contact page is not required for V1.

## Homepage Structure

1. Header and navigation
2. Hero introduction
3. Current focus
4. Featured projects
5. Latest writing
6. Learning journey
7. Short about section
8. Footer and professional links

## Project States

- Completed — Crypto Risk Intelligence Lakehouse
- Ongoing Series — Talos Agent Lab
- In Development — Job Application Copilot
- Building in Public — Engineering Portfolio

## Public Information Guardrails

- Do not publish private contact details.
- Do not expose home addresses, identification numbers or immigration information.
- Do not publish confidential employer or customer information.
- Do not invent project outcomes, statistics or technical experience.
- Review the résumé before making it publicly downloadable.
