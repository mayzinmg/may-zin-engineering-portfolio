# Resume and Contact Privacy

## Public Contact Details

The portfolio may display:

- Professional email address
- LinkedIn profile
- General location: Singapore

## Details Not Published

The portfolio and downloadable résumé must not expose:

- Phone number
- Street or residential address
- Identification numbers
- Date of birth
- Private references
- Signatures
- Private travel or availability details

## Downloadable Résumés

The portfolio will provide two privacy-safe PDF résumés:

1. Data & Analytics Résumé
   - For data engineering, analytics engineering and Azure data-platform roles.

2. AI Applications Résumé
   - For AI-enabled applications, senior software engineering, backend and cloud roles.

The generic résumé will not be published because the targeted versions communicate each professional direction more clearly.

Before publication, both files must be reviewed to ensure that:

- only approved public contact details appear;
- phone number, work-pass status and sponsorship details are removed;
- document metadata does not reveal private information;
- all employment and certification claims are accurate;
- external links are valid;
- selectable text remains readable by recruiters and applicant-tracking systems.

## Contact Behaviour

- Email will use a `mailto:` link.
- LinkedIn will open as an external link.
- No contact form or backend service is required for V1.
