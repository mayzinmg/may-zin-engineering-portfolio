# Writing Page Brief

## Purpose

The Writing page will provide a structured index of my published articles and ongoing learning series.

## V1 Publishing Model

Medium remains the source of the full articles. The portfolio will display article summaries and link visitors to the original publications.

## Topics

- Data Engineering
- Agentic AI
- Software Engineering
- Books
- Travel
- Films

## Article Card Information

Each published article may contain:

- Title
- Topic
- Short description
- Publication date
- Reading time, when known
- External article link

## Guardrails

- Do not display unpublished articles as completed work.
- Do not invent publication dates or reading times.
- Clearly identify external links.
- Do not expose private drafts or personal travel schedules.
- Every link must be reviewed before deployment.
