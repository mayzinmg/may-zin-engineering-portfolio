
After applying for jobs for quite some time, I have come to realise that it is important to present my skill set, visa status, employment history, projects and professional insights in one structured place. This will allow recruiters and me to assess suitability more efficiently and avoid wasting each other’s time. Therefore, I am creating a portfolio website to present my professional profile while learning and applying vibe-coding principles, including AI steering, iterative development, security guardrails and human-in-the-loop review.

## Target Audience

The primary audience for this portfolio is fellow technology professionals with whom I can share ideas and exchange feedback, allowing the portfolio and its projects to improve through subsequent iterations. The secondary audience includes anyone who wants a clearer picture of my professional background, technical interests and current work.

## Intended Outcomes

Through this project, I intend to familiarise myself with agentic AI and vibe-coding principles and practices. At the same time, visitors should be able to explore my projects, learning journey, current areas of focus and progress in one place.
## V1 Scope
The first version of the portfolio will allow visitors to:

- Explore my professional experience.
- Read detailed explanations of the scope, tools and lessons learned from completed projects.
- Follow the progress and documentation of ongoing projects.
- Access my Medium articles about data engineering, AI, books, travel experiences and films.
- Access my GitHub and LinkedIn profiles through clearly identified links.
